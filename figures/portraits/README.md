# Historical portrait sources

The book includes 27 small, unmodified JPEG reproductions, acquired on
2026-10-27 from Bookofproofs revision
`c81f01462158ad7cdd2ea0c9b5090c674c635588` under its CC BY-SA 4.0 notice.
The licence applies separately from the book's original CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 text.
See [third-party notices](../../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) and the exact URLs,
hashes, and source-gallery links in [sources.json](sources.json).

Each caption cites its individual University of St Andrews/MacTutor image
gallery and the reproduction supplier. These galleries were opened during
research. They identify the sitters and provide visual source material, but
**they do not consistently identify the original maker, date, or physical
object**. Those original-object details remain unverified unless stated below.
Do not describe every image as acquired directly from a museum or as having
fully established primary-object provenance. No modern drawings or generated
likenesses were used. The native images are small, so the book prints them
at compact margin size without enlarging them into full-page portraits.

Single portraits fill the margin width. Paired portraits each use 49% of
that width with a 2% gap; no small height cap reduces their displayed width.
Native proportions are preserved, and the margin scheduler keeps each image
group and its caption together.

## Supplementary primary and custodian records

These are leads and checked object metadata, **not assertions that a different
scan in the ledger was downloaded from that institution**. Match the complete
image against the original object before replacing its image-source citation.

- **L'Hôpital:** [MSK Gent, inventory 2015-EL](https://www.mskgent.be/en/collection/2015-el)
  identifies an engraving by Gerard Edelinck after **Nicolas Fouché**, and
  labels it public domain. The MacTutor gallery instead says “after Jean
  François Foucher (1761–1831)”; do not propagate that conflicting attribution.
- **Newton:** [Isaac Newton Institute, Kneller portrait](https://www.newton.ac.uk/about/art-artefacts/portrait-of-isaac-newton/)
  is a custodian source for the familiar 1689 Kneller painting. Its page was
  inaccessible to direct fetch; the linked source appeared in the reproduction
  metadata. An exact reproduction match remains to be completed.
- **Johann Bernoulli:** [University of Tübingen portrait record via LEO-BW](https://www.leo-bw.de/fr/detail/-/Detail/details/DOKUMENT/ubt_portraits/101563/Bernoulli%20Johann)
  identifies Johann Jakob Haid and dates the print before 1742. The [Basel
  portrait credit](https://hls-dhs-dss.ch/de/articles/019087/2020-09-11/)
  describes a Haid mezzotint after Johann Rudolf Huber, published in 1742.
  Do not confuse Johann I with Johann II or Johann III.
- **Cauchy:** [Smithsonian SIL-SIL14-C2-02](https://library.si.edu/image-gallery/73509)
  describes a Rudolf Hoffmann lithograph; [SIL-SIL14-C2-04](https://library.si.edu/image-gallery/73510)
  is a different, Reutlinger photograph. Select and match the relevant object;
  a shared sitter is insufficient to establish provenance.
- **Weierstrass:** [Smithsonian portrait record](https://library.si.edu/image-gallery/73709)
  and the [Smithsonian's first-party Flickr reproduction](https://www.flickr.com/photos/smithsonian/2552869153/)
  document an historical portrait. The source pages did not establish a match
  to the small reproduction used here.
- **Riemann:** [Smithsonian portrait record](https://library.si.edu/image-gallery/73213).
- **Bolzano:** [Czech Digital Mathematics Library portrait](https://www.dml.cz/handle/10338.dmlcz/400449)
  is a digitised historical portrait, not a modern reconstructed face.
- **Fourier:** [Smithsonian portrait records 73409](https://library.si.edu/image-gallery/73409)
  and [73410](https://library.si.edu/image-gallery/73410) provide alternatives.
- **Dedekind:** [ETH image archive, Portr 11953](https://doi.org/10.3932/ethz-a-000046496)
  is a Johannes Ganz portrait from about 1862. It is a different, younger
  portrait from the later likeness reproduced here; do not cite it as the
  source of the book's current image.
- **Cantor:** [Library of Congress, 2005685241](https://www.loc.gov/pictures/item/2005685241/)
  holds a photogravure whose date is unrecorded and whose rights status is
  unevaluated. It is distinct from the portrait of the entertainer Eddie
  Cantor returned by broad catalogue searches. Exact print matching remains
  pending.
- **Heine:** [Oberwolfach Photo Collection, photo 10540](https://opc.mfo.de/detail?photo_id=10540&suggest=1)
  identifies an image from the Hamburg Mathematical Society's photo album.
  This is Eduard Heine, not the poet Heinrich Heine.
- **Bernstein:** [University of Halle professor catalogue](https://www.catalogus-professorum-halensis.de/bernsteinfelix.html)
  is an institutional lead for Felix Bernstein. A source for an original
  photograph should still be established.

## Portrait exceptions

No contemporary likeness is asserted for Euclid, Archimedes, or Leonardo of
Pisa. Their existing historical notes should not be accompanied by an
unlabelled later imagined face. Joseph Raphson also has no verified portrait
in this collection. A named person in a bibliography is not a request to add
a separate portrait of a modern editor or historian.

## Reuse

Retain the sitter, image-source citation, supplier credit, and CC BY-SA 4.0
notice with reproductions. If a larger or better documented source replaces a
file, update its caption key, ledger, licence, and hash together. Preserve the
aspect ratio and identify any cropping, retouching, or colour changes. The
current files are byte-identical to the supplier files.
