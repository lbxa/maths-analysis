\chapter{Sets and foundations of real analysis}
\label{ch:analysis-i:sets-and-foundations}

\input{figures/interval-styles}
\input{figures/foundations/styles}

This chapter moves from counting sets to filling gaps in the
rational line, then from completeness to the functions built on the real
numbers. The sequence of arguments matters. Counting reveals how large
$\R$ is; completeness explains why the limits and roots we need exist.

\autoref{sec:analysis-i:sets-and-countable-sets}--\autoref{sec:analysis-i:power-sets}
compare sets and identify gaps in $\Q$.
\autoref{sec:analysis-i:convergence-preview} introduces distance, limit
rules, and the motivation for iteration.
\autoref{sec:analysis-i:fields-and-order}--\autoref{sec:analysis-i:completeness-equivalences}
develop order and completeness, including the Archimedean property before
the proof chain uses it.
\autoref{sec:analysis-i:archimedean}--\autoref{sec:analysis-i:roots-and-powers}
apply these tools to approximation, missing limits, functions, and roots.
The iteration proofs return at the end, when their prerequisites are in place.
