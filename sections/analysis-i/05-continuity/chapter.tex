\chapter{Continuity}
\label{ch:analysis-i:continuity}

\begin{pendingreview}
Continuity turns small changes in an input into small changes in its output.
We begin with this local condition, then use subsequences and completeness
to prove that continuous functions attain extrema and intermediate values.
On a closed bounded interval, uniform continuity also lets us approximate
the whole graph by straight segments.
\end{pendingreview}
