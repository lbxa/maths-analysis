\section{Differentiation}
\label{sec:analysis-i:differentiation}

\begin{pendingreview}
Differentiation studies local change through the limiting slope of a
secant line.
\historynote{Newton's 1669 manuscript links infinite series with
quadrature; Leibniz's 1684 paper publishes rules for differentials
and tangents~\cite{Newton1669,Leibniz1684}. Their approaches developed
in the seventeenth-century study of motion, areas, and tangents.
These primary texts document distinct contributions. The modern limit
definition came later. Calculus transformed mechanics
and differential equations. See \autoref{fig:portrait:leibniz}.}
