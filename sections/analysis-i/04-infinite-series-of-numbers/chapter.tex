\chapter{Infinite series of numbers}
\label{ch:analysis-i:infinite-series-of-numbers}

A series converges when its sequence of partial sums converges.
We begin with this definition and its Cauchy criterion, then use
geometric series to control more complicated sums. Absolute convergence
provides that control even when the terms have different signs.
