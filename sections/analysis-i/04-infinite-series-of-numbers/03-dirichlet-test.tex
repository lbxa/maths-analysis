\section{Dirichlet test}
\label{sec:analysis-i:dirichlet-test}
\historynote{Dirichlet's 1829 paper sought conditions under which a
trigonometric series really represents a function~\cite{Dirichlet1829}.
Controlling cancellation, rather than absolute values alone, is the
idea behind the test bearing his name. Its impact is especially clear
in Fourier analysis. The general series-test statement is reserved
for the later mathematical treatment.}
