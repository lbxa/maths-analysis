\section{Upper and lower sums}
\label{sec:analysis-i:upper-and-lower-sums}

\begin{pendingreview}
Upper and lower sums bound an accumulated quantity from above and below
on each interval of a partition.
\historynote{Darboux's 1875 \emph{M\'emoire sur les fonctions
discontinues} develops upper and lower integrals
~\cite{Darboux1875}. These bounds make integrability a question
of whether the gap can be made arbitrarily small. This viewpoint
clarifies Riemann's theory and the role of discontinuities, and
provides a practical way to prove error estimates. See \autoref{fig:portrait:darboux}.}
\historyportrait{darboux}{Gaston Darboux}{PortraitDarboux}

\end{pendingreview}
