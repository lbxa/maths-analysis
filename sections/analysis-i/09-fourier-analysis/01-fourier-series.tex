\section{Fourier series}
\label{sec:analysis-i:fourier-series}

\begin{pendingreview}
Fourier series represent functions using trigonometric modes. The
representation and its convergence require separate questions.
\historynote{Fourier's \emph{Th\'eorie analytique de la chaleur}
(1822) uses trigonometric expansions to solve heat-conduction problems
~\cite{Fourier1822}. The physical problem was to follow an initial
temperature distribution over time. This programme connected analysis
with partial differential equations and became foundational for
frequency methods in physics and signal analysis. See \autoref{fig:portrait:fourier}.}
\historyportrait{fourier}{Joseph Fourier}{PortraitFourier}

\end{pendingreview}
