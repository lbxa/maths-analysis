\section{Convergence of Fourier series}
\label{sec:analysis-i:convergence-of-fourier-series}

\begin{pendingreview}
A formal trigonometric expansion needs hypotheses before it can be
identified with the function it is meant to represent.
\historynote{Dirichlet's 1829 paper establishes convergence under
specific conditions and examines shortcomings in earlier arguments
~\cite{Dirichlet1829}. Riemann later revisited representation and
integration~\cite{Riemann1867}. This sequence of problems explains
why Fourier analysis helped drive more careful definitions of
function, continuity, and convergence.}
\end{pendingreview}
