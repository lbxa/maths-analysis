\section{Metric spaces}
\label{sec:analysis-i:metric-spaces}

\begin{pendingreview}
A metric abstracts the distance properties used on the real line, so that
convergence can also be studied for points, sequences, and functions.
\historynote{Fr\'echet's 1906 thesis studies convergence in collections
of mathematical objects, including functions~\cite{Frechet1906}.
Its abstract distance framework helped move analysis beyond individual
real or complex variables. Metric spaces provide a common language
for approximation, continuity, and functional analysis. See \autoref{fig:portrait:frechet}.}
\historyportrait{frechet}{Maurice Fr\'echet}{PortraitFrechet}

\end{pendingreview}
