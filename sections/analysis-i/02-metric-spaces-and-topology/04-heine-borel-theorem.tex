\section{Heine--Borel theorem}
\label{sec:analysis-i:heine-borel-theorem}

\begin{pendingreview}
The Heine--Borel theorem connects a covering property with closedness and
boundedness in finite-dimensional Euclidean space.
\historynote{Heine's 1872 treatment uses finite interval coverings;
Borel's 1895 thesis uses a covering argument in function theory
~\cite{Heine1872,Borel1895}. The modern compactness theorem grew
through these related steps. Its impact is to reduce infinitely many
local requirements to finitely many choices. Closed and bounded sets
need not be compact in an arbitrary metric space. See \autoref{fig:portrait:borel}.}
\historyportrait{borel}{\'Emile Borel}{PortraitBorel}

\end{pendingreview}
