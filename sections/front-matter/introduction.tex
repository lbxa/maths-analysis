\chapter*{About these notes}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{About these notes}

These notes follow the course outline for Introduction to Mathematical
Analysis I and II. The foundations chapter develops the first three weeks
from Lucas Barbosa's handwritten lecture notes, preserving their progression
from sets and cardinality to completeness, density, and rational powers.
Proofs have been expanded where the handwritten arguments were abbreviated.

The main text contains the definitions and complete arguments. Margin
notes provide reminders, secondary observations, and small diagrams.
Examples quoted from later mathematics are identified when their proofs
are outside the present scope.

Later chapters still contain marked specimens from the supplied
Tufte-\LaTeX{} sample; they are scaffolding for future course notes.
Their historical software instructions are not project instructions.
Contributors should follow \texttt{CONTRIBUTING.md} for this project's
workflow. Analysis II remains reserved for later.
