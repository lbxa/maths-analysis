\chapter*{About these notes}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{About these notes}

This book develops the foundations of mathematical analysis.
The foundations chapter progresses from sets and cardinality to
completeness, density, and rational powers.
The chapters on sequences and number series develop convergence,
with power-series examples in the chapter on sequences and series
of functions. Results stated without proof are explicitly identified.

The main text contains the definitions and complete arguments. Margin
notes provide reminders, secondary observations, and small diagrams.
Examples quoted from later mathematics are identified when their proofs
are outside the present scope.

\chapter*{Introduction}
\label{ch:introduction-to-analysis}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Introduction}

% Reserved for the author's broad introduction to analysis and common tools
% and techniques to know before beginning the course material.
\begin{pendingreview}
Analysis studies how infinite processes interact with arithmetic and
geometry. A sequence may approach a value without reaching it, and a
series may have a finite sum despite having infinitely many terms.
Completeness explains why the limiting values required by these
processes exist in the real numbers.

The distinction between existence and computation runs throughout this
book. A proposed formula for a limit does not show that the sequence
converges. A drawing may suggest that a curve reaches a value, while
continuity and completeness provide the justification. We will repeatedly
turn such suggestions into arguments by choosing a tolerance, bounding
an error, and showing that the bound works wherever the conclusion
requires it.

The foundations chapter introduces the set, order, and limit tools used
later. The subsequent chapters develop sequences, series, continuity,
and differentiation within the book's stated assumptions. Some later
sections remain outlines. Their headings preserve the intended
progression, and a historical introduction does not replace a missing
definition or proof.
\end{pendingreview}
